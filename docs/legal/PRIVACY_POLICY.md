# Telly — Privacy Policy
*Last Updated: October 2, 2026*

Telly ("we", "our", or "us") provides a social television ranking platform via our mobile applications and websites. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Apple App Store Privacy Guidelines.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Data:** When you register, we collect your authentication credentials (Apple ID token, Google OAuth token, or Phone Number for SMS verification), username, and display name.
- **Entertainment Data:** Your TV show rankings, pairwise duel decisions, watchlists, completed seasons, tags, and micro-reviews.
- **Streaming Configuration:** Active streaming service subscriptions you select (e.g. Netflix, Max) to personalize streaming availability.
- **Device & Telemetry Data:** IP address, device model, operating system version, and anonymized in-app interactions collected via privacy-first analytics (PostHog). We **do not** use IDFA or third-party ad-tracking SDKs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- To generate your personalized TV canon, dynamic percentile ratings, and leaderboard tiers.
- To compute **Taste Match %** rank correlations with other users.
- To power the collaborative **"Two-to-Watch"** co-watching recommendation engine.
- To alert you when shows on your queue are leaving streaming platforms or premiering new seasons.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **Public Visibility:** By default, your username, display name, and completed TV canon are visible to other users. You can enable **Friends-Only Mode** or **Ghost Mode** in Settings at any time.
- **Third-Party Services:**
  - **TMDB (The Movie Database):** Show metadata and cast information.
  - **JustWatch:** Streaming availability lookups.
  - **Twilio:** Secure delivery of SMS verification codes.
  - **Supabase:** Cloud database and authentication hosting.
- We **never sell your data** to advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 4. User Rights & Account Deletion (GDPR / CCPA)
You have the full right to access, export, or delete your data:
- **Data Export:** You can export your entire TV history to CSV or Notion format directly from the Settings menu.
- **Account Deletion:** You can delete your account at **Settings $\rightarrow$ Delete Account**. This initiates an immediate deactivation followed by permanent, irreversible cryptographic deletion after 30 days.
