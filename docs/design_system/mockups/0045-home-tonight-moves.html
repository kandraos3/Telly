Home: Tonight and your moves

The approved Home layout (option B of three; see decision 0011). A hero card shows what to watch next, followed by up to four one-tap moves and a single friends line. The canon top 3 is no longer on Home. Movies and series stay separate wherever a ranking appears.

The example user is in the middle of three series on a Tuesday evening, finished The Bear yesterday, and is on a 6-week streak.

Approved · option B

Tonight: one big "watch this next" card, then your moves

Home answers one question first: what do I watch now? Below that is a short, ranked list of things worth doing, with a single row of friends. Your canon leaves Home (owner's call), since the Canon tab is one tap away.

  • Hero: your most recent tracked title with its next episode and ✓ Watched E6. With nothing tracked it falls back to your Queue's Up next pick, then to "Start your canon" for a new user. Smaller chips underneath switch between the titles you're watching.
  • Your moves: up to 4 cards chosen by a small, testable Dart ranker: rank a finished title, a new season is out, your streak needs a ranking by Sunday, a challenge is close to done, a friend ranked something you've also ranked. Each card has one action.
  • Friends: one line with faces and the latest thing, which opens Social.
  • The catch: the moves ranker is new logic to design and test, and what Home shows changes day to day, which some people find less predictable.
