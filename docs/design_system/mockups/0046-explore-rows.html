Engine E3 · draft weights, fixed in the spec

How a pick is scored

fit = 0.40 · taste      cosine of the title's genres vs your canon's genre profile,
                        each genre weighted by your scores (not a raw count)
    + 0.20 · seeds      how many of your top-ranked titles TMDB links it to, and how high
    + 0.15 · friends    friends who ranked it, weighted by their taste match with you
    + 0.15 · quality    Telly community score, else TMDB rating (shrunk toward the mean)
    + 0.10 · services   1 if it streams on a service you have, else 0

Shown as "92% match". Rows take titles in order, skipping any title already shown,
with at most 2 per franchise or director per row.
Something different flips taste: high quality and good friends' scores, low genre overlap.
Movies and series are scored separately (dual canon).
