Telly website: five chapters with a playable duel

The owner chose direction A (five chapters: Track, Rank, Discover, Friends, Play) with a playable duel between two highly rated shows as the hero. The mock uses example content and simplified screens. The real site renders its screenshots from the app's own widgets, and the spec (docs/design_system/05_WEBSITE.md) wins where they differ.

01 · Track

Always know what's on tonight.

Home opens on the next episode of whatever you're watching. Tick episodes off as you go, and Telly keeps your place in every show.

Tonight

Episode progress

Seasons, caught-up shows and what's new.

02 · Rank

Duels, not stars.

Finished something? Pick which you liked more, a few times. Binary search finds its exact place, and your rank becomes a score from 1.00 to 10.00.

  • Movies only duel movies; series only duel series
  • A podium for your top three in each ranking
  • Share the score reveal straight to your story
    03 · Discover

    Find the next one without scrolling for an hour.

    Explore builds rows from your own rankings and your friends' favourites. Save anything to your queue, which knows where it's streaming.

    Up next

    One pick from your queue when you can't decide.

    Two-to-Watch

    Titles you'll both enjoy, on the services you share.

    04 · Friends

    Upsets worth arguing about.

    See what friends rank as they rank it. Telly flags the upsets, Taste Match shows who really shares your taste, and squads merge everyone into one leaderboard.

    Find friends

    Search by name. Keep your profile public or friends-only.

    Squads

    A shared ranking for the group chat or film club.

    05 · Play

    Watching, with a scoreboard.

    Earn medals for what you've watched, finish collections, join monthly challenges with your squad, and level up every week you keep your streak.

    • Gold, silver, bronze and special medals
    • Weekly quests and a friends' table
    • Rewards that unlock custom header art
