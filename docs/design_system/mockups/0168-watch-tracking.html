Epic #168 · Decision 0010 · Approved mockup

Tracking on the title page

Option A (the episode pointer), reworked so tracking runs through the whole title page instead of sitting in one card, with its own Tracking hub, a Canon presence and a shorter Home card. The +1 button is gone. All titles, friends and numbers are example content.

What changed since round 1
  • The page knows where you are. While you watch, the header says "Watching · S2 · E6 next" and the backdrop carries a thin progress line.
  • Start watching joins the action row. Add to Queue becomes a single Watch slot that moves Queue → Watching → Finished.
  • Next episode replaces Streaming now. One card holds the episode's still, name and runtime, ▶ Watch on Apple TV+ and ✓ Watched E6.
  • Seasons become your progress. Each season shows a ring and its state. Inside a season, episodes before yours are ticked, yours is marked You're here, and later synopses are blurred to avoid spoilers. Tapping any episode moves your place there.
  • Friends and the community join in. "Watching now: Maya, Jordan" (no episode numbers), and the survival rate tells you if you're past the episode where most people drop it.
  • A Tracking hub. A new Watching screen opened from a tile in More (under Queue) and from Home's See all. Home now shows a 3-row summary only.
  • The Canon shows it. A Watching strip above the podium for the selected canon, a progress tag on ranked rows you're still watching, and an Episodes this year stat.
  • Caught up is its own state. When you're up to date on an airing show, the page shows when the next episode or season arrives.

Seasons with progressThe pointer + tv_seasons.episode_countNo per-episode rows. Ticks are worked out from your place.
Spoiler blurThe pointerUses the same rule as Spoiler Shield: reveal on tap.
