Gamification directions

Three ways to make Telly a little more of a game. They lead with different things: what you've collected, how you've levelled up, or what you're doing with friends right now. They can be combined, so the decisions at the bottom matter more than picking one letter. Frames are a 375 × 760 dp phone at 80%. Names, titles and numbers are examples.

App theme
A

Trophy case

Achievements lead. You collect medals for milestones, finished film collections (TMDB collections such as The Dark Knight Trilogy), and taste moments like a big upset. Pin three to your profile. A weekly streak sits quietly alongside. It's calm, personal and easy to understand, with friends only one tap away.

  • Fits a ranking app: it celebrates what you've watched instead of pushing volume.
  • Collections give a clear "what next" (the missing films, one tap to Queue).
  • Little day-to-day pull on its own. Medals come slowly.
  • Needs TMDB collection data, which we don't fetch yet.

B

Levels and weekly quests

Points lead. Ranking, finishing collections and weekly quests earn XP. XP raises your level, and levels unlock cosmetic rewards such as profile frames, share-card styles and app icons. A weekly friends' table adds light competition. This is the closest to your "points that unlock things" idea.

  • Strongest weekly habit loop: three fresh quests every Monday.
  • One number to compare with friends, and rewards to aim for.
  • Points for ranking can push people to log things they didn't watch, which hurts the canon. Needs caps and rules.
  • Overlaps with Telly Pro (#52) if rewards ever become features.

C

Challenges and marathons

Time-boxed challenges lead: "Spooktober: 8 horror films by Oct 31", "The Batman marathon", or a challenge your squad sets for itself. You join, see who else is in, and race your friends to the finish. Finishing earns a medal. This is your "movie marathon" idea, built around doing things together.

  • Most social: built-in reasons to talk in squads (#49) and the feed.
  • Seasonal challenges keep it fresh without daily nagging.
  • Needs someone to write challenges every month (or a set of templates).
  • Lonely for people without friends on Telly yet (friends land in #48).

Decide later

The calls that shape it

None of these needs an answer today. My recommendation is in each card.

1. What leads?

  1. A: Trophy case (collect)
  2. B: Levels and quests (progress)
  3. C: Challenges (do it with friends)

Recommend: A as the base, C next, and B's points only as a light layer later. Achievements are the calmest fit for a ranking app. Challenges then add the social pull.

2. What do points unlock?

  1. Nothing: no points, only medals
  2. Cosmetics: frames, card styles, icons
  3. Features: things like advanced stats

Recommend: cosmetics. Feature unlocks compete with Telly Pro (#52) and make free users grind for basics.

3. Streak rhythm

  1. Weekly, with one freeze a month
  2. Daily, like Duolingo
  3. No streak

Recommend: weekly with a freeze. People don't finish a film every day, so a daily streak would feel like a punishment.

4. Who you compete with

  1. People you follow and your squads
  2. Everyone (global leaderboards)
  3. Nobody: personal only

Recommend: friends and squads only, with weekly tables that reset. Global boards reward grinding and invite cheating. A "rare: 4% of viewers" line gives the global flavour without a table.

5. Protecting the canon

  1. Only count titles that went through duels
  2. Weekly cap on points for ranking
  3. No points for ranking at all

Recommend: 1 and 2 together. Rewards should never make it worth ranking something you didn't watch.

6. How public it is

  1. Unlocks post to the feed by default (can turn off)
  2. Off by default
  3. Never in the feed

Recommend: on by default for medals and finished challenges only (not every quest), with a privacy toggle in Settings.

If you go with the recommendation
  1. Slice 1: achievements (milestones and taste medals), the weekly streak, pin to profile, the unlock moment.
  2. Slice 2: collections from TMDB (needs a backend change), then seasonal and squad challenges.
  3. Slice 3, optional: XP, levels and cosmetic rewards, once you've decided on Telly Pro.
