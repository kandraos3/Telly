# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on [kandraos3/Telly](https://github.com/kandraos3/Telly/security/advisories/new). Don't open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. You'll get a reply within a week.

In scope: the Telly app, its Supabase backend (database policies, RPCs, edge functions) and this repository's workflows. Out of scope: third-party services Telly calls (TMDB, Supabase itself); report those to their owners.
